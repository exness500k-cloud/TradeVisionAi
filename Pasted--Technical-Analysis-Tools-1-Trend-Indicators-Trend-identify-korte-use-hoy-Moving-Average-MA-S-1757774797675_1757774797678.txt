 Technical Analysis Tools
1️⃣ Trend Indicators
Trend identify korte use hoy:
Moving Average (MA) – Simple (SMA) / Exponential (EMA)
MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence) – Trend + momentum
Parabolic SAR – Trend reversal points
---
2️⃣ Momentum Indicators
Momentum measure kore, overbought/oversold signals dey:
RSI (Relative Strength Index) – 0–100 scale, overbought >70, oversold <30
Stochastic Oscillator – Overbought/oversold, momentum
CCI (Commodity Channel Index) – Price deviation from average
---
3️⃣ Volatility Indicators
Market-er volatility measure kore:
Bollinger Bands – Price range + volatility
ATR (Average True Range) – True volatility measure
---
4️⃣ Volume Indicators
Price movement confirm korte use hoy:
OBV (On-Balance Volume) – Buying/Selling pressure
Volume Oscillator – Volume trend detect
---
5️⃣ Support / Resistance & Others
Fibonacci Retracement / Extension – Support & resistance zones
Pivot Points – Intraday support/resistance levels
